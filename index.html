Open the AI Pal

Tap the PingPal icon whenever you need help with food, questions, research, ideas, and more.

2

Chat Like a Friend

You'll land in a private chat room. Just type your question or request, and PingPal answers.

3

Let It Remember You

PingPal remembers your past conversations, so over time it learns what you like and what you've researched. That means better, more personal recommendations — no need to repeat yourself.

4

Talk Instead of Typing

Prefer to speak? Use voice commands to chat with PingPal hands-free, like an Alexa that's specialized in recommendations.

5

Make It Yours

PingPal is the default name, but you can customize it to whatever you like.
